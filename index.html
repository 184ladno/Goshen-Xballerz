THE GOSHEN APPROACH

Built around every player

Focused coaching that develops the person behind the player.

    01 / PERSONAL DEVELOPMENT

    1-to-1 coaching

    Personalised sessions to sharpen technique, build confidence and accelerate development.

    02 / SMALL GROUP TRAINING

    Small groups

    Focused, high-quality sessions in a small group environment with players of similar ability.

    03 / PLAYER MINDSET

    Mindset & decision making

    Develop the thinking behind better decisions, composure and consistent performance.

    04 / A CLEAR PLAN

    The session pathway

    Each session moves through three purposeful stages, helping players practise, apply and reflect.

    1. Technical practiceBuild and refine core skills with purposeful repetition.
    2. Game-related challengesApply skills in realistic, pressured situations.
    3. ReflectionReview, learn and set clear next steps.
